Machining and repair for the North Atlantic fleet

Straumnes Marine makes shafts, propeller parts, winch components and hydraulic fittings for fishing vessels and fish processing plants. We work from our harbour workshop in Hafnarfjordur.

Our services

CNC machining

Turning and milling of shafts, couplings, flanges and bushings up to six metres in length. We machine steel, stainless steel, bronze and aluminium, as single parts or in series.

Vessel repair

Our service team repairs propulsion, steering and deck machinery at the quay, often on the same day. When a vessel must go back to sea quickly, we work around the clock.

Processing equipment

Spare parts and stainless steel components for fish processing lines, made to drawing or copied from a worn part.

Who we work for

Our customers are fishing companies, shipowners and fish processing plants in Iceland, the Faroe Islands and Greenland. Many of them have worked with us for more than twenty years, and our engineers know their vessels well.

Quality

  • Certified to ISO 9001
  • Approved workshop for the main classification societies
  • Material certificates and a measurement report for each part
