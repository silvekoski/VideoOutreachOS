# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mergero's origination analysts manage seller prospects from Pipedrive. Company owners in the Nordics and DACH receive personalized videos and can book a meeting. These users and workflows are specified in PRD.md.

## Product Purpose

Help analysts turn prospect company names into remote meetings through reviewed, personalized videos, engagement information, and follow-up tasks.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, precise, and personal. Keep Mergero's existing artwork and brand identity. The PRD calls for compact controls and a restrained, precise interface.

## Anti-references

The PRD rejects generic AI templates, stock photography, and AI imagery. Use actual company websites, buyer logos, and analyst recordings.

## Design Principles

- Keep the analyst's review and outreach workflow direct.
- Preserve Mergero branding and fixed, predictable layouts.
- Use real prospect material and clear, short text.
- Make the prospect experience work on mobile phones and across supported languages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect prefers-reduced-motion, as required by the PRD. Preserve accessible labels, keyboard navigation, and the current responsive layout. Fonts must support the Nordic and German characters listed in the PRD.
